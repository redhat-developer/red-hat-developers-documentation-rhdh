= Assess the impact of breaking changes before migrating
:context: phase-1-assess-breaking-changes

[role="_abstract"]
When you are planning an upgrade from {product} 1.10 to 2.1, you want to understand every breaking change and its impact on your deployment, so you can scope the work, assign it to the right people, and avoid unexpected failures during migration.

This phase helps you review the breaking changes, inventory your current configuration, and complete a pre-upgrade checklist to prepare for the migration.
