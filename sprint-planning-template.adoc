== Tips for Effective Sprint Planning

. *Pre-work:* Run readiness checks on candidate tickets before the meeting
** Use: `check if RHIDP-XXXXX is ready to work on`
** Flag any with delivery risks

. *Timeboxing:* Stick to agenda times - defer deep technical discussions

. *JIRA hygiene:* Ensure tickets have:
** Clear acceptance criteria
** Parent epic/feature links
** Fix version set
** SME identified

. *Risk flags:* Call out:
** Tickets with "New" or "In Progress" sibling epics (delivery timing risk)
** Tickets waiting on engineering PRs
** Tickets with blocked dependencies

. *Buffer capacity:* Don't commit 100% - leave room for urgent bugs/clarifications
