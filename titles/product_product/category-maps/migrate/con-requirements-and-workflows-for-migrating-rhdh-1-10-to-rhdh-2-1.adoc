= Requirements and workflow for migrating {product-very-short} 1.10 to {product-very-short} 2.0

[role="_abstract"]
Understand what breaks, what changed, and how to migrate plugins, so that you can complete a {product-short) 1.10 to {product-very-short} 2.0 migration without downtime or silent failures.

To migrate from {product-short) 1.10 to {product-very-short} 2.0, you should use the following workflow:

* *Assess breaking changes* — Review the summary of breaking changes and identify which ones apply to your deployment.                                                       
* *Prepare custom plugins* — Update custom plugins for the New Frontend System and inventory Helm values, RBAC policies, and configuration.
* *Reinstall the platform* — Uninstall RHDH 1.10 and perform a clean installation of RHDH 2.1 using the standalone Helm chart.                                               
* *Update configuration* — Update RBAC permissions, authentication providers, and other configuration to match renamed services and new conventions.                         
* *Verify migration* — Test your RHDH 2.1 deployment, verify plugin functionality, and resolve common migration issues.
