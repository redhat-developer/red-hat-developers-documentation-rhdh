= Register and discover AI content assets across teams

[role="_abstract"]
Use the `AIContext` entity kind to register source repositories and OCI images that contain AI skills, rules, and agent definitions, and make them discoverable in the {product} ({product-very-short}) Software Catalog.

[IMPORTANT]
====
The `AIContext` entity kind is early, evolving content related to {product} capabilities. It is authored by {company-name} experts but has not yet been tested on every supported configuration, and its schema is experimental and might change. The structure, depth, and level of detail might vary, and information might be updated, reorganized, or removed based on product changes and user feedback. Refer to the feature-specific link:https://access.redhat.com/support[support] scope for details.
====
