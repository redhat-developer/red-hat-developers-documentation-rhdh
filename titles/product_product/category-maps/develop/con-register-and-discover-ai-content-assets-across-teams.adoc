= Register and discover AI content assets across teams

[role="_abstract"]
As a developer, use the `AIContext` entity kind to register source repositories and OCI images that contain AI skills, rules, and agent definitions, and to discover assets that other teams publish in the {product} ({product-very-short}) Software Catalog.

include::{docdir}/artifacts/snip-developer-preview.adoc[]

If you are a Platform Engineer who wants to publish governed, authoritative AI content assets for your organization, see the governance guidance in the Configure documentation.
