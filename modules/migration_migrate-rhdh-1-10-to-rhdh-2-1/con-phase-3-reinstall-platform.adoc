= Move your platform to the {product} 2.1 standalone Helm chart

[role="_abstract"]
When you move your production {product} instance to 2.1, you must export your current configuration, translate your values to the new standalone chart structure, and upgrade the release in place. This lets you run the platform with the correct container images without uninstalling the 1.x release.

This phase helps you understand the Helm chart changes, translate your configuration values, update container image references, and upgrade the platform.
