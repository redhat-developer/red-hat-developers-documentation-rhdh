= Migrate AI assistant configuration by using the Helm chart

[role="_abstract"]
In {product} {product-version}, the AI assistant is renamed from {rhdeveloper-name} Lightspeed for {product} to {ia-brand-name}. To migrate by using the Helm chart, update your Helm values, app-config keys, and RBAC policies, and reconfigure Llama Stack connections to LCORE.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Helm values and app-config use different naming. In the Helm chart, the keys use camel case, for example `intelligentAssistant.{asterisk}`, and some settings are restructured rather than simply renamed. In app-config, the keys and route paths use `intelligent-assistant`. Do not apply a single find-and-replace of `lightspeed` across both surfaces.
====

.Prerequisites

* You have completed the platform migration and installed the 2.1 Helm chart.
* You have access to your {product} configuration files and RBAC policies.

.Procedure

. Update your Helm values:
.. Map the `global.lightspeed.{asterisk}` values to the `intelligentAssistant.{asterisk}` structure.
.. Some settings are restructured, not just renamed. For the complete mapping, see the {ia-name} section of the xref:ref-helm-chart-values-mapping-1-x-to-2-1_{context}[Helm chart values mapping].

. Rename AI assistant app-config keys:
.. Replace `lightspeed` with `intelligent-assistant` in your `app-config` keys.
.. Update route path references from `/lightspeed` to `/intelligent-assistant`.

. Update RBAC policies:
.. Replace old `lightspeed.{asterisk}` permission names with the {product} 2.1 permission names, for example `intelligent-assistant.chat`, and change the action to `use`.
.. Refer to the xref:ref-ai-permissions-mapping-1-x-to-2-1_{context}[AI permissions mapping] for the complete list of permissions.
.. For the full RBAC policy update procedure, including conditional policies, see xref:proc-update-rbac-policies-for-rhdh-2-1_{context}[Update RBAC policies for {product-very-short} 2.1].

. Reconfigure Llama Stack connections:
.. Update any Llama Stack connection settings to point to LCORE instead.

.Verification

. Verify that AI assistant features respond as expected.
. Confirm that RBAC policies grant the correct access.
