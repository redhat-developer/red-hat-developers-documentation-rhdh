= Migrate AI assistant configuration

[role="_abstract"]
To migrate your AI assistant configuration to {product} 2.1, update your Helm values to the `intelligentAssistant.{asterisk}` structure, rename your app-config keys from `lightspeed` to `intelligent-assistant`, update RBAC policies, and reconfigure Llama Stack connections to point to LCORE.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Helm values and app-config use different naming. In the Helm chart, the keys use camelCase, for example `intelligentAssistant.{asterisk}`, and some settings are restructured rather than simply renamed. In app-config, the keys and route paths use `intelligent-assistant`. Do not apply a single find-and-replace of `lightspeed` across both surfaces.
====

.Prerequisites

* You have completed the platform migration and installed the 2.1 Helm chart.
* You have access to your {product} configuration files and RBAC policies.

.Procedure

. Update your Helm values:
.. Map the `global.lightspeed.{asterisk}` values to the `intelligentAssistant.{asterisk}` structure. Some settings are restructured, not just renamed.
.. For the complete mapping, see the Intelligent Assistant section of the xref:ref-helm-chart-values-mapping-1-x-to-2-1_{context}[Helm chart values mapping].

. Rename AI assistant app-config keys:
.. Replace `lightspeed` with `intelligent-assistant` in your `app-config` keys.
.. Update route path references from `/lightspeed` to `/intelligent-assistant`.

. Update RBAC policies:
.. Replace old `lightspeed.{asterisk}` permission names with the {product} 2.1 permission names, for example `intelligent-assistant.chat`.
.. Refer to the xref:ref-ai-permissions-mapping-1-x-to-2-1_{context}[AI permissions mapping] for the complete list of permissions.

. Reconfigure Llama Stack connections:
.. Update any Llama Stack connection settings to point to LCORE instead.

.Verification

. Verify that AI assistant features respond as expected.
. Confirm that RBAC policies grant the correct access.
