= Record {ia-name} settings to prevent migration data loss

[role="_abstract"]
To migrate without losing customizations, record your {product-very-short} 1.10 settings and back up your configuration before you deploy {product} ({product-very-short}) 2.1.

Keep the 1.10 deployment available until 2.1 verification finishes, so that you can roll back if the migration fails.

.Prerequisites
* You have administrative access to your {product-very-short} 1.10 deployment.
* You have access to your deployment method configuration: your Helm values file or your {backstage} custom resource (CR).
* You have an approved secret-management process for storing credentials.

.Procedure
. Record the installed {product-very-short} and {ia-name} versions.
. Back up your 1.10 Helm values or your {backstage} custom resource (CR).
. Export your customer-managed ConfigMaps, Secrets, `app-config` files, dynamic plugin configuration, role-based access control (RBAC) policies, Routes, Services, and persistent volume claims.
. Record the {ia-name} settings that you must reapply in 2.1: enabled inference providers and their model names, Model Context Protocol (MCP) server endpoints, query validation behavior, feedback and chat history storage settings, and prompt customizations and storage requirements.
. Store credentials through your approved secret-management process. Do not add decoded Secret values to your migration workspace.

.Verification
. Confirm that you have a stored copy of your 1.10 Helm values or {backstage} CR.
. Confirm that you have a recorded list of the {ia-name} settings that you must reapply in 2.1.
. Confirm that your 1.10 deployment remains available for rollback.
