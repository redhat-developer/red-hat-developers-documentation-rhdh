= Create isolated research workspaces

[role="_abstract"]
Organize your work into individual notebook sessions to keep research topics separate and private. Notebooks are available in overlay, docked, and fullscreen display modes.

image::integrate_interacting-with-developer-lightspeed-for-rhdh/lightspeed-notebook-fullscreen-page.png["Notebook fullscreen page"]

.Procedure
. In the {product-very-short} interface, click the *Intelligent Assistant* button.
. In your *{ls-short}* page, select the *Notebooks* tab.
. Click *Create a new notebook* to start a new workspace.
+
The notebook opens with an empty resource panel. In overlay or docked mode, click the toggle icon to show or hide the resource panel.
+
[NOTE]
====
When you close a notebook, the system automatically saves or discards it based on the following rules:

* *Save*: The notebook name has been edited, even if no resources have been added.
* *Save*: Resources have been added, even if the name remains "Untitled notebook".
* *Save*: Both the name has been edited and resources have been added.
* *Discard*: The notebook name is unedited and no resources have been added.
====

. Optional: To manage your workspaces:
** *Rename*: Double-click the notebook name to edit it inline. Press *Enter* to save the new name or *Escape* to cancel. Alternatively, hover over the notebook card, click the *More options* icon, and select *Rename*.
** *Delete*: Hover over the notebook card, click the *More options* icon, and select *Delete*.
+
image::shared/lightspeed-notebook-menu-icons.png["Notebook menu with icons"]

.Verification
* Confirm the new notebook card appears on the *My Notebooks* dashboard. Each card displays the notebook name, the resource count (for example, "1 resource", "No resources", or "5 resources"), and the last updated date.
