= Save and reuse chat prompts to avoid retyping

[role="_abstract"]
To reuse instructions that you enter frequently, save them as prompts in {ia-name} and apply them in any chat session without retyping or pasting them. Saved prompts are private to your account and available across the overlay, docked, and fullscreen chat modes.

.Prerequisites
* You have configured the {ia-name} plugin in {product}.
* You have logged in to the {product-very-short} console.

.Procedure
. Click the *Open intelligent assistant* floating action button (FAB) at the lower right of the screen to open the chat overlay.

. Create a saved prompt:
.. Click the *Chatbot options* icon (⋮) in the header and select *MCP and Prompt Settings*.
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-chatbot-options.png["Chatbot options menu in the header with MCP and Prompt Settings highlighted"]
.. Click the *Saved prompts* tab.
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-saved-prompts-empty.png["Empty Saved prompts tab with the New prompt button"]
.. Click *+ New prompt*.
.. Enter a *Title* of 128 characters or fewer and a *Prompt* of 5,000 characters or fewer.
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-new-prompt-form.png["New prompt form with Title and Prompt fields on the Saved prompts tab"]
.. Click *Save*.
+
[NOTE]
====
By default, you can save up to 100 prompts. When you reach the limit, delete an existing prompt before you create a new one.
====

. Reuse a saved prompt from the *Saved prompts* section of the chat history sidebar or from the *Saved prompts* tab:
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-saved-prompts-menu.png["Saved prompts tab and sidebar section with a prompt's options menu open on Apply in input box, Send directly, and Delete"]
+
--
* To insert the prompt into the message field so that you can review or edit it before sending, click the prompt, or select *Apply in input box* from the prompt's options menu.
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-apply-in-input-box.png["Apply in input box selected in the prompt's options menu, with the prompt inserted in the message field"]
* To insert and submit the prompt immediately, select *Send directly* from the prompt's options menu.
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-send-directly.png["Send directly option in the saved prompt options menu"]
--
+
[NOTE]
====
When the sidebar lists more than five saved prompts, click *Show all* to display the remaining prompts, or click *Show less* to collapse the list.
====

. Optional: To remove a saved prompt, select *Delete* from the prompt's options menu, and then confirm the deletion. Deleting a saved prompt is permanent.
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-delete-prompt.png["Confirmation dialog for permanently deleting a saved prompt"]

. Optional: To open the *Saved prompts* settings directly, click the settings (gear) icon next to the *Saved prompts* section in the chat history sidebar.
+
image::shared/intelligent-assistant-chat-saved-prompts-settings.png["Saved prompts settings gear in the sidebar and the opened Saved prompts settings tab"]

. Optional: To show or hide the *Saved prompts* feature, click the *Chatbot options* icon (⋮) in the header and toggle *Enable saved prompts* or *Disable saved prompts*. Saved prompts are enabled by default. When you disable the feature, {product-very-short} hides the *Saved prompts* section and excludes your saved prompts from the default prompts offered in a new chat.

.Verification
. The *Saved prompts* tab displays the prompt count and your new prompt.
. Clicking or sending a saved prompt applies its content in the message field.
. Deleted prompts are removed from both the sidebar and the *Saved prompts* tab.
