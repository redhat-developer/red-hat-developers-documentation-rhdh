= Save and reuse chat prompts to avoid retyping

[role="_abstract"]
To reuse instructions that you enter frequently, save them as prompts in {ls-short} and apply them in any chat session without retyping or pasting them. Saved prompts are private to your account and available across the overlay, docked, and full-screen chat modes.

.Prerequisites
* You have configured the {ls-short} plugin in {product}.
* You have logged in to the portal.

.Procedure
. Click the *Open intelligent assistant* floating action button at the lower right of the screen to open the chat overlay.

. Create a saved prompt:
.. Click the *Chatbot options* icon (⋮) in the header and select *MCP and Prompt Settings*.
.. Click the *Saved prompts* tab.
.. Click *+ New prompt*.
.. Enter a *Title* of 128 characters or fewer and *Prompt content* of 5,000 characters or fewer.
.. Click *Save*.
+
[NOTE]
====
By default, you can save up to 100 prompts. When you reach the limit, delete an existing prompt before you create a new one.
====

. Reuse a saved prompt from the *Saved prompts* section of the chat history sidebar or from the *Saved prompts* tab:
+
--
* To insert the prompt into the message field so that you can review or edit it before sending, click the prompt, or select *Apply in input box* from the prompt's options menu.
* To insert and submit the prompt immediately, select *Send directly* from the prompt's options menu.
--
+
[NOTE]
====
When the sidebar lists more than five saved prompts, click *Show all* to display the remaining prompts, or click *Show less* to collapse the list.
====

. Optional: To change a saved prompt, select *Edit* from the prompt's options menu, update the *Title* or *Prompt content*, and then click *Save*.

. Optional: To remove a saved prompt, select *Delete* from the prompt's options menu, and then confirm the deletion. Deleting a saved prompt is permanent.

. Optional: To show or hide the *Saved prompts* feature, click the *Chatbot options* icon (⋮) and toggle *Enable saved prompts* or *Disable saved prompts*. Saved prompts are enabled by default. When you disable the feature, {product-very-short} hides the *Saved prompts* section and excludes your saved prompts from the default prompts that are offered in a new chat.

.Verification
. The *Saved prompts* tab displays the prompt count and your new prompt.
. Clicking or sending a saved prompt applies its content in the message field.
. Edited and deleted prompts are reflected in both the sidebar and the *Saved prompts* tab.
