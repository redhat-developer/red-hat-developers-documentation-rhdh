= HTTP/2 TLS requirements

[role="_abstract"]
Verify that your environment meets the TLS requirements before you enable HTTP/2 for {product-very-short}.

Your environment must meet the following requirement to enable HTTP/2:

Valid TLS certificate:: You must obtain the TLS certificate from a certificate authority (CA) that the browser trusts. Certificates that the browser does not trust do not enable HTTP/2 negotiation in most browsers.
