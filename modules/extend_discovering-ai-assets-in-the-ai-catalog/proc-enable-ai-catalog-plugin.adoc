= Enable the AI Catalog plugin

[role="_abstract"]
To allow developers to discover AI assets, enable the AI Catalog dynamic plugin in your {product-short} instance.

.Prerequisites
* You have administrator access to your {product-short} instance.
* AI Asset entities are ingested into the Software Catalog.
* The entities include the required annotations: `rhdh.io/ai-asset-category`, `rhdh.io/ai-asset-version`, and `rhdh.io/ai-asset-source`.

.Procedure
. Edit your `dynamic-plugins-rhdh` ConfigMap to include the AI Catalog plugin:
+
[source,yaml,subs="+quotes"]
----
kind: ConfigMap
apiVersion: v1
metadata:
  name: dynamic-plugins-rhdh
data:
  dynamic-plugins.yaml: |
    includes:
      - dynamic-plugins.default.yaml
    plugins:
      - package: '@redhat/backstage-plugin-ai-catalog'
        disabled: false
----

. Optional: Configure filter visibility in your `app-config.yaml` or `app-config-rhdh` ConfigMap:
+
[source,yaml,subs="+quotes"]
----
app:
  extensions:
    - entity-ref: 'plugin:ai-catalog'
      config:
        filters:
          owner:
            disabled: true  # Hide the owner filter
----

. Apply the updated ConfigMap:
+
[source,terminal,subs="+quotes"]
----
$ oc apply -f __<configmap-file>__
----
+
where `__<configmap-file>__` is the path to your updated ConfigMap YAML file.

. Restart the {product-short} pods to load the plugin:
+
[source,terminal,subs="+quotes"]
----
$ oc rollout restart deployment/backstage
----

.Verification
. Navigate to your {product-short} instance in a web browser.
. Confirm that an *AI Catalog* menu item is displayed in the navigation sidebar.
. Click *AI Catalog* and verify that AI assets are displayed in the card grid.
