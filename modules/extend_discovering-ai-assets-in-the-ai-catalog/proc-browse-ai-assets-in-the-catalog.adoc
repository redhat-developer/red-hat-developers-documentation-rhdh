= Browse AI assets in the catalog

[role="_abstract"]
To find AI capabilities for your application, browse the AI Catalog using search, filters, and category grouping.

.Prerequisites
* The AI Catalog plugin is enabled in your {product-short} instance.
* AI assets are registered in the Software Catalog.

.Procedure
. Navigate to your {product-short} instance and click *AI Catalog* in the sidebar.
+
The AI Catalog landing page displays all AI assets you are authorized to view, grouped by category.

. Optional: Search for assets by name, description, or tag:
.. Type keywords into the search box at the top of the page.
.. Results update as you type, showing only assets matching your search terms.

. Optional: Filter assets by category, source, or tags:
.. Open the filter sidebar on the left side of the page.
.. Select one or more categories: *AI Agents*, *AI Skills*, *MCP Servers*, *AI Models*, or *Model Servers*.
.. Select one or more source connectors to filter by origin: *MCP Registry*, *{rhoai-short}*, *JFrog*, or *Solo Agentregistry*.
.. Select tags or labels to narrow results further.
.. Click *Clear filters* to reset all filter selections.

. Optional: Combine search and filters to narrow large result sets:
.. Type keywords in the search box.
.. Apply filters in the sidebar.
.. The catalog displays only assets matching both your search terms and selected filters.

. Review asset cards in the grid:
+
Each card displays the following information:
+
. Asset name
. Short description
. Category icon or label
. Source connector
. Current or recommended version
. Owner (if visible)

. Click an asset card to view its detail page.

.Verification
. Assets matching your search terms and filter selections appear in the catalog grid.
. The catalog shows a "no results" message if no assets match your current filters.
