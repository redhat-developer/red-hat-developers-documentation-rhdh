= Configure AI Catalog filters

[role="_abstract"]
To control which filters appear in the AI Catalog sidebar, configure filter visibility in your application configuration.

.Prerequisites
* You have administrator access to your {product-short} instance.
* The AI Catalog plugin is enabled.

.Procedure
. Edit your `app-config-rhdh` ConfigMap to configure filter visibility:
+
[source,yaml,subs="+quotes"]
----
app:
  extensions:
    - entity-ref: 'plugin:ai-catalog'
      config:
        filters:
          owner:
            disabled: true
          lifecycle:
            disabled: false
----
+
where:
+
`owner`:: Disables the owner filter, hiding it from the filter sidebar.
`lifecycle`:: Enables the lifecycle filter, showing it in the filter sidebar.

. Apply the updated ConfigMap:
+
[source,terminal,subs="+quotes"]
----
$ oc apply -f __<configmap-file>__
----

. Restart the {product-short} pods:
+
[source,terminal,subs="+quotes"]
----
$ oc rollout restart deployment/backstage
----

.Verification
. Navigate to the AI Catalog in your browser.
. Confirm that the configured filters appear or do not appear in the sidebar based on your settings.

The following table lists the available filters:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Filter | Description

| `category`
| Filters by AI asset category (AI Agents, AI Skills, MCP Servers, AI Models, Model Servers)

| `source`
| Filters by source connector (MCP Registry, {rhoai-short}, JFrog, `Solo Agentregistry`)

| `tags`
| Filters by tags and labels applied to assets

| `owner`
| Filters by asset owner (optional, can be disabled)

| `lifecycle`
| Filters by asset lifecycle status (optional, can be disabled)
|===
