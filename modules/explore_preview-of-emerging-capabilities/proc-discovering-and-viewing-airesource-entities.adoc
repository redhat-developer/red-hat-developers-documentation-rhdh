= Discover and view AiResource entities

[role="_abstract"]
Discover registered AI content assets by filtering the Software Catalog by kind or by using global search, and view the full asset metadata on the entity detail page.

.Prerequisites

* You are logged in to the {product-very-short} instance.
* At least one `AiResource` entity is registered in the catalog.

.Procedure

. To list all AI content assets, click *Catalog* in your {product} navigation menu, click the *Kind* drop-down list, and select *AiResource*.
+
The catalog lists each `AiResource` entity with its name, asset type, owner, description, and asset location.
. To find a specific asset, enter a term that appears in the asset name, description, or tags in the global search field, such as `python`, `security`, or `test-writing`.
+
Matching `AiResource` entities appear in the search results alongside other entity kinds.
. Click the name of an `AiResource` entity to open its detail page.
. On the entity detail page, review the asset metadata, including the asset type, owner, description, and links.
. Click the asset location link to open the source repository URL or to view the OCI image reference.
. Optional: If the entity has a `backstage.io/techdocs-ref` annotation, click the *Docs* tab to view the linked documentation in the TechDocs viewer.
. Optional: View the relationships graph on the entity detail page to see how the asset relates to other catalog entities.

.Verification

* You can locate a registered `AiResource` entity by both the kind filter and global search, and open its detail page.
